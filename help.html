❓ Help & Documentation

How do I edit a mistake on a Service Entry?
Navigate to the Engineer Jobs tab from the Home Menu. You will see a list of your past jobs. Click the blue "Edit" button next to the job, fix the error, and click "Save".
Why does the Dashboard say "Offline"?
The dashboard pulls live data from the master cloud server. If it says Offline, check your phone's internet/data connection and refresh the page.
I forgot my password or cannot log in.
If you are locked out, please contact the System Administrator to have your password reset in the master directory.

Need Technical Support?

Contact the MIS Department via internal email or call the IT helpdesk during standard business hours.
